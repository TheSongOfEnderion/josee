Before her was a group of 26 or so slaves, mostly women who were cowering in fear.

"P-please don't kill us!"
"We are just slaves... huhhu"
"Please great mage!"

As the moon stood up high, she pulled her hood revealing her face.

Josee glared at the cowering women, causing them to shriek in fear.

"... If you wish to survive", Josee spoke, "then use your hands and feet"

"... You have eyes and ears. I'm sure all of you knows. Bring me all the golds and treasure these scums hid. Leave not a single one, and you will be safe"

They looked at one another before looking back at her.

"... Do we have an undestanding?", Josee glared.

""Y-Yes!"", all, of them shouted in unison.

"... Pass my words to the others hiding in the island. Running away is fine, I can simply catch you **all**"

The girls immediately ran away, each with coldness running down their spine. But as they ran, a single person was left behind just seating at a stool.

It was an old lady with a kind smile on her face. She was wearing a humble off-white garment with bright colored seashell around the neck, a clothing in stark contrast with what the slaves were wearing.

'Who the hell is this granny?', Josee wondered. Recalling back in her memories, she did not remember anything.

"... Grandma", Josee spoke, "What are you doing here?"

"Ahh, do not mind me my child", the old lady spoke softly. Her voice almost soothing to the ears.

"I am nothing more than a humble fortune teller"

"... Really?", Josee raised her eyebrows, "... You can tell fortunes? Is that how these pirates are so successful?"

Josee also wondered. How are these guys able to evade the powers that surounded these lands? It couldn't simply be for mere navigation skills, else they would have voyaged the fjord islands for greater riches.

"Indeed they are...", the old woman calmly spoke, "My abilities to foresee glimpses of the future  me to guide them to plunder"

"... Why help them then?", Josee asked.

"Have you seen how I look my child?", the old lady replied, "I am frail and in the last years of my life. My family is gone and no one left to take care of me. In exchange for taking care of me, I give my service"

"... Interesting", Josee crossed her arms.

"... So", Josee coldly asked, "...why didn't you warned them about me?"

"Hehe", the old lady laughed. Her eyes opened a bit, giving josee a glimpse of the dim and yet cold blue eyes hiding in her slits.

"Why should I?", She replied in a cold voice.

Josee felt a shiver down her spine. Somehow this old lady was the scariest person on this island.

"... Hmmm", Josee pondered.

'... A person that can see glimpses of the future is certainly a potent ally. But can she be trusted? A sly fox has no allegiance.'

Josee closed her eyes.

'... Well, she is dying after all. She wasn't there when this island was attacked by the male lead so she must have died shortly'

"There is no need to overthink it", the old lady stood up with a staff in her hand.

"I am old and have seen all there is to see", she spoke with her eyes closed.

"If you would take care of me for my last year, I would lend you my service. But I don't want any more innocent be harmed. Do we have a deal?"

Vishka nodded.

"Oh, on the giant rock besides the Shipwreck", the old lady shouted, "that's where they hide the good stuff"
